Free features and Timary Pro

Appointments, clients, services, history, iCloud and data transfer remain available without a subscription. Optional Timary Pro opens client messages and templates, detailed statistics and date selection, calendar details, reminder customization, and selecting days and exporting available-hours images. Viewing today’s available hours and creating a story preview remain free. Monthly and annual subscriptions provide the same Pro features.

Prices, trials and renewal

The App Store shows the price, currency, billing period and any eligible introductory trial before you confirm. Offers depend on your Apple Account and storefront; a trial is not available to everyone. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled. After a trial, the displayed subscription price is charged unless you cancel.

Restore purchases

Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. In Timary, open Settings → Pro → Unlock full access, then choose Restore Purchases on the purchase screen. This also restores previously purchased lifetime access. If access is still missing, check your App Store account and contact support without sending payment credentials.

Apple Calendar

Open Settings → Apple Calendar, allow full access and create a Timary calendar or select an existing one. Enable sending on only one device. Choose iCloud to view events on other devices. Changes made in Apple Calendar do not update Timary and will be replaced with Timary data. If you move sending to another device, turn it off on the old one first, then select the same calendar on the new one.

Manage or cancel a subscription

In Timary, open Settings → Pro → Manage Subscription, or open your device Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Timary. Cancel a trial at least 24 hours before it ends to avoid renewal. Deleting Timary does not cancel the subscription. Canceling renewal normally keeps paid access until the end of the current period. Apple’s cancellation instructions.

Offer codes and refunds

If neither Pro nor a current subscription is active, Settings → Pro shows Redeem promo code when access has been checked. Apple handles redemption and eligibility; access opens after StoreKit verifies the purchase. For a refund request, use Apple’s Report a Problem service; Apple decides eligibility.

When you write

Include your Timary version, iOS or iPadOS version, device model and the steps that led to the issue. You can find the app version at the bottom of Settings. Please redact client names, contact details and notes from screenshots.

Reminders

Check that notifications are allowed for Timary in device settings, then review the reminder settings in the app. Reminders notify you; Timary does not automatically send messages to clients.

iCloud sync

Use the same Apple Account on your devices and enable sync in Timary on each one. Fully close and reopen the app after changing the setting. Turning sync off does not delete your iCloud data.

Backups and moving devices

Open Settings → Timary Data to export a backup or restore a file. Restoring replaces the current app data. Keep an extra copy before restoring and protect backup files because they contain client information.

Privacy requests

Use the same contact email for questions about data, access or deletion. The developer cannot directly browse records held only on your device or in your private iCloud storage. We can help you find the appropriate controls.
